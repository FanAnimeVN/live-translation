## 1. What are the current (2026) VND price anchors for digital subscriptions in Vietnam?

### Takeaway
Most foreign digital subscriptions rose about 5% from 1 Jul 2025, when VAT on foreign digital services went from 5% to 10%. Several rose again in 2026 (iCloud+ +20–32% in July 2026). The cheapest well-known paid tiers now sit at 33k–79k VND/month: Spotify Student 33k, YouTube Premium Lite 49k (web), Spotify 65k, Netflix Mobile 74k, VieON VIP ~79k. Consumer AI plans sit at ~122k–132k (Google AI Plus, ChatGPT Go at launch). The earlier proposal's anchors need updating. Netflix Mobile ~74k still holds. The "YouTube Premium 79k" figure is stale or disputed, and the more useful YouTube anchor is now Premium Lite at 49k (web) / 65k (app).

### Inferences
- **Anchor ladder for "Đọc 39k / Cày 99k":**
  - Below 39k: data day packs (5–10k), Spotify Student (33k).
  - Around 39–49k: YouTube Premium Lite web (49k).
  - 59–79k: Spotify (65k), YouTube Lite in-app (65k), Netflix Mobile (74k), VieON VIP (~79k), MangaToon VIP (~US$1.99 ≈ 52k).
  - 99–132k: FPT Play VVIP (130k), Google AI Plus (122–132k), ChatGPT Go at launch (132k).
  - 39k is still the cheapest "real" paid tier among familiar brands. 99k still sits just under the consumer-AI price points.
- **Psychological gaps:** 49k is now a visible YouTube price point. Pricing "Đọc" at 49k would put it level with YouTube Lite rather than under it, so 39k keeps a clear gap. 99k stays under the ~120–130k AI-plan cluster.
- **Web vs app pricing:** YouTube charges 49k on the web vs 65k in the app, and older reports cite similar gaps for Premium. Buyers in VN are used to web prices being cheaper than in-app prices. This supports selling on the team's own website rather than through app stores.
- **VAT in competitors' prices:** foreign services' VND prices now include 10% VAT. If the team operates as a household business under the 1-billion-VND threshold (Q3), it owes no VAT. Advertised prices may not need to "include VAT" in the same sense [verify].

## 2. What is the best payment stack for a small team at beta and at launch (fees, settlement, onboarding, recurring, refunds)?

### Takeaway
For VN buyers, VietQR bank transfer with automatic confirmation is the cheapest and easiest option for a student team. payOS (free, sign-up with one CCCD citizen ID) or SePay (free up to 50 transactions/month, then 120k VND/month, no per-transaction fee) settle straight into the team's own bank account and suit prepaid passes and credits. VietQR has no auto-debit. MoMo has a tokenized "Subscription" API for opt-in recurring billing, but its business gateway requires a registered business, and its fees are unpublished (third-party sources say 1–3%; via Adyen 3% + 3,650 VND). Stripe is not available to Vietnamese entities. Merchant-of-record (MoR) services that do pay out to Vietnam (Polar, Lemon Squeezy, Dodo Payments, and Paddle, which does not exclude VN) charge ~5% + US$0.50 plus ~1.5% for international cards. On a 39k ticket that is ~40%, so they only make sense for foreign or USD-priced annual purchases.

- **payOS** (by Casso; homepage fetched 21 Oct 2026):
  - A "free payment gateway" built on VietQR, with costs subsidized by banks. — [payOS](https://payos.vn/)
  - The plan shown is "Pro / BIDV-1K": free, 1,000 transactions/year, unlimited payment channels. A BIDV promo runs to 31 Dec 2026 for business customers (free BIDV-1K, a digital signature, up to 6 Soundboxes). — [payOS](https://payos.vn/)

## 3. What legal and tax obligations apply in Vietnam in 2026?

### Takeaway
2026 brought large changes:
- **Tax:** presumptive tax (thuế khoán) and the business-license fee ended on 1 Jan 2026, and households now self-declare on actual revenue. The VAT and PIT exemption threshold for household and individual businesses was raised to **1 billion VND/year**, retroactive to 1 Jan 2026 (Decree 141/2026/NĐ-CP). Businesses must report the bank and e-wallet accounts they use for business to the tax authority.
- **E-commerce:** a new E-commerce Law (122/2025/QH15) and Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP took effect on 1 Jul 2026. A site that sells its own services is a "direct-business platform". It must publish pricing/fees, payment, termination and refund policies and a privacy policy. Notification of direct-business platforms with online ordering appears to be handled at the provincial level.
- **Privacy:** the Personal Data Protection Law (91/2025/QH15) and Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP took effect on 1 Jan 2026. Small firms get exemptions, but these are lost if they process "sensitive" data, and Decree 356 reportedly classes behavioural/online-activity tracking as sensitive. That may capture reading history [verify].
- **Consumer law:** ongoing services must remind users ≥7 working days before expiry, and consumers may cancel at any time.

### Cited Findings

#### Business registration
- Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP Art. 82(3) exempts certain people from household-business registration: farming/fishing households, street vendors, mobile or seasonal traders, and "low-income services". The exemption does not cover conditional business lines. — [LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/nhung-truong-hop-khong-can-phai-dang-ky-ho-kinh-doanh-a168708.html); [Thư viện Pháp luật](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/ma-so-thue/phap-luat-thue/huong-dan-trinh-tu-thu-tuc-dang-ky-ho-kinh-doanh-online-2026-481158-225550.html)
- Selling online is not itself an exemption. Regularity and scale decide whether registration is needed. — [Kế toán Thuận Thiên](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/hoi-dap/ban-hang-online-facebook-tiktok-co-bat-buoc-dang-ky-ho-kinh-doanh-khong/)
- Household-business registration can be filed online under Decree 168/2025 (Art. 113). Since 1 Jul 2025 it is filed together with tax-code registration. — [Thư viện Pháp luật](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/ma-so-thue/phap-luat-thue/huong-dan-trinh-tu-thu-tuc-dang-ky-ho-kinh-doanh-online-2026-481158-225550.html)

#### Tax for households / individual businesses (2026)
- From 1 Jan 2026, under Resolution 198/2025/QH15, household businesses moved from presumptive tax to self-declaration on actual revenue. — [Thuế Hải quan / Tạp chí Kinh tế Tài chính](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/chuyen-sang-ke-khai-tu-2026-ho-kinh-doanh-tinh-thue-nhu-the-nao-134770.html)
- The business-license fee (lệ phí môn bài) was abolished from 1 Jan 2026 [secondary]. — [Kế toán Thuận Thiên](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/kien-thuc/hieu-lam-ve-thue-ho-kinh-doanh/)
- **Decree 141/2026/NĐ-CP** (issued 29 Apr 2026, effective retroactively from 1 Jan 2026) — [Tạp chí Công Thương, 1 May 2026](https://tapchicongthuong.vn/chinh-thuc--nang-nguong-chiu-thue-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-len-1-ty-dong-nam--ap-dung-tu-1-1-2026-514336.htm):
  - Amends Decree 68/2026/NĐ-CP and Decree 320/2025/NĐ-CP.
  - Raises the non-taxable revenue threshold for household businesses and individual business operators from 500 million to **1 billion VND/year**.
  - Enterprises with total annual revenue ≤1 billion are exempt from corporate income tax.
  - Households above 1 billion must use tax-authority-coded e-invoices (or cash-register e-invoices). They must register within 30 days after the period in which cumulative revenue passes the threshold.
- Corroborating coverage: about 2.5 million households fall under the 1-billion line, and households under it are not obliged to use e-invoices. — [SGGP](https://www.sggp.org.vn/nang-nguong-chiu-thue-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-len-1-ty-dongnam-post850439.html); [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/ho-kinh-doanh-co-doanh-thu-duoi-1-ty-dong-chua-bat-buoc-dung-hoa-don-dien-tu.htm); [Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/nghi-dinh-141-2026-nd-cp)
- Earlier coverage cited 500 million under the amended PIT Law passed 10 Dec 2025 (Law 109/2025/QH15); Decree 141/2026 superseded that figure [secondary]. — [Kế toán Thuận Thiên](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/kien-thuc/hieu-lam-ve-thue-ho-kinh-doanh/); [Tạp chí Công Thương](https://tapchicongthuong.vn/chinh-thuc--nang-nguong-chiu-thue-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-len-1-ty-dong-nam--ap-dung-tu-1-1-2026-514336.htm)
- The PIT rate on revenue is 2% for "services, construction without materials" and 0.5% for goods distribution. It applies to the band above the threshold up to 3 billion, per Decision 3389/QĐ-BTC (2025) [secondary university note; verify method]. — [Duy Tân University note](https://files01.duytan.edu.vn/svruploads/kketoan-duytan/639041981258209295-bai-web-thang-1.2026-nhu.docx)
- Secondary sources say household VAT is computed on total revenue at the sector rate, without deducting the threshold [verify]. — [Thuế Hải quan](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/chuyen-sang-ke-khai-tu-2026-ho-kinh-doanh-tinh-thue-nhu-the-nao-134770.html)
- **Bank accounts:** household businesses and individual business operators must notify the tax authority, electronically, of all bank/payment accounts and e-wallets used for business (Decree 68/2026/NĐ-CP). — [Thời báo Tài chính VN, 6 Mar 2026](https://thoibaotaichinhvietnam.vn/ho-kinh-doanh-phai-thong-bao-so-tai-khoan-nhan-tien-kinh-doanh-cho-co-quan-thue-193246.html)
- The form is reported as Mẫu 01/BK-STK, filed via the tax e-portal or eTax Mobile [secondary]. — [Kế toán Thuận Thiên](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/hoi-dap/ban-hang-online-nho-le-thi-nen-dung-tai-khoan-ca-nhan-hay-bat-buoc-mo-tai-khoan-kinh-doanh/)
- **Is an AI service a "software service"?** Tax offices have treated AI services as not software services, so they are subject to 10% VAT (Hà Nội Tax sub-department 6 on ChatGPT). This matters for how a VN AI translation service is classified if it becomes VAT-liable. — [VnReview](https://vnreview.vn/threads/chatgpt-co-tinh-la-dich-vu-phan-mem-va-duoc-mien-thue-vat-khong-co-quan-thue-dua-ra-cau-tra-loi.83371/latest); [Crowe Vietnam](https://www.crowe.com/vn/vi-vn/news/purchasing-chatgpt-of-openai-with-only-an-invoice)

#### E-commerce rules for a site selling its own service
- **Law 122/2025/QH15** was passed 10 Dec 2025 and took effect 1 Jul 2026. Decree 248/2026/NĐ-CP (30 Jun 2026) guides it. — [Tạp chí Công Thương, 3 Jul 2026](https://tapchicongthuong.vn/bo-cong-thuong-pho-bien-luat-thuong-mai-dien-tu-va-nghi-dinh-so-248-2026-nd-cp-531199.htm); [Báo Lào Cai](https://baolaocai.vn/tu-hom-nay-172026-ban-hang-online-khong-the-an-danh-nguoi-ban-can-lam-gi-post902924.html)
- The law defines four platform types: direct-business, intermediary, social network with e-commerce, and integrated. A **direct-business platform** is one set up by an organization or individual to sell its own goods or services (Art. 3). — [Law 122/2025 EN text, Thư viện Pháp luật](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/EN/Thuong-mai/Law-122-2025-QH15-Electronic-Commerce/703876/tieng-anh.aspx)
- **Notification:** in Sơn La's implementation plan, the provincial People's Committee (advised by the Department of Industry and Trade) receives the administrative procedure for notifying direct-business e-commerce platforms with online ordering. — [LSVN, 14 Aug 2026](https://lsvn.vn/son-la-trien-khai-thuc-hien-luat-thuong-mai-dien-tu-a177539.html)
- **Art. 11 (public disclosure):**
  - All platforms must prominently publish, in Vietnamese: operator details, a privacy policy, the parties' rights and obligations, and how complaints are handled.
  - Platforms with online ordering must also publish prices and all fees, sales limits and payment policy. For services they must publish how the service is provided, the termination policy and the refund policy.
  - Users must signal agreement to this information before opening an account.
  - Source: [Hệ thống Pháp luật, Art. 11](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-thuong-mai-dien-tu-2025/dieu-11)
- **Art. 16 (direct-business operators):**
  - Disclose service information, and keep data on posted services accessible for ≥1 year.
  - With online ordering, keep contract information for ≥3 years. Micro-enterprises, household businesses and innovative SME startups need only ≥1 year, for up to 5 years after founding.
  - Source: [Hệ thống Pháp luật, Art. 16](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-thuong-mai-dien-tu-2025/dieu-16)

#### Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)
- **Law 91/2025/QH15** took effect 1 Jan 2026. Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP (issued 31 Dec 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026) guides it and replaces Decree 13/2023. — [Rajah & Tann](https://vn.rajahtannasia.com/?p=63882); [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/nghi-dinh-356-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-422896-d1.html)
- **Small-business relief:**
  - Small enterprises and startups get a 5-year grace period from 1 Jan 2026 before they must prepare impact-assessment dossiers and appoint data-protection staff. Household businesses and micro-enterprises are exempt from both.
  - The relief does **not** apply if processing reaches ≥100,000 data subjects, if the business provides data-processing services, or if it directly processes sensitive data.
  - Source: [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/new-decree-provides-guidance-for-vietnams-personal-data-protection-law)
- Consent cannot be a default or given through ambiguous instructions. — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/new-decree-provides-guidance-for-vietnams-personal-data-protection-law)
- Consent must be specific, clearly expressed and stored, and the controller bears the burden of proving it. — [EY Vietnam alert (VI), Mar 2026](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/vi-vn/technical/tax/documents/ey-vietnam-legal-alert-march-2026-decree-no356-2025-nd-cp-providing-detailed-guidance-for-implementation-of-personal-data-protection-law-viet.pdf)
- **Sensitive-data scope:** per EY, Decree 356 adds to the sensitive category data that tracks behaviour and activity in telecom, social network, online media and other cyberspace services. It removes online activity history from the "basic data" group [verify against Art. 4 of the decree]. — [EY Vietnam alert (VI)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/vi-vn/technical/tax/documents/ey-vietnam-legal-alert-march-2026-decree-no356-2025-nd-cp-providing-detailed-guidance-for-implementation-of-personal-data-protection-law-viet.pdf)
- **DPO and assessments:** the data-protection officer needs ≥2 years' experience in legal, IT, cybersecurity or HR plus PDP training. There are new templates for processing and cross-border impact assessments, and the Ministry of Public Security reviews submissions within 15 days. — [PwC Vietnam, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.pwc.com/vn/en/publications/2026/20260128-new-rules-personal-data-protection.pdf)
- Cross-border transfer agreements must state the legal basis and each side's responsibilities. — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/new-decree-provides-guidance-for-vietnams-personal-data-protection-law)
- **Penalties:**
  - Cross-border transfer violations: up to 5% of the previous year's revenue or 3 billion VND, whichever is higher.
  - Data trading: up to 10× the illegal gains/revenue.
  - Other violations: up to 3 billion VND; individuals face half these amounts.
  - Sources: [Tuổi Trẻ News](https://news.tuoitre.vn/trading-personal-data-faces-tenfold-fines-under-vietnams-new-privacy-law-103260101125618158.htm); [Vietnamnet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/data-security-breaches-could-cost-firms-5-of-annual-revenue-2539258.html); [Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/report/vietnams-new-personal-data-protection-law-imposes-administrative-fines-up-to-5-of-annual-revenue-for-non-compliance/)
- **Cybersecurity Law 2025** (Law 116/2025/QH15) took effect 1 Jul 2026. EY summarizes a requirement to store user data in Vietnam; the exact scope for small domestic firms is unclear. — [EY Vietnam](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/luat-an-ninh-mang-2025-cua-viet-nam-cac-diem-chinh-va-yeu-cau-tuan-thu)

#### Consumer protection (auto-renewal, cancellation, refunds)
- **Law 19/2023/QH15** (issued 20 Jun 2023) defines "remote transactions" as transactions made online where the consumer cannot inspect the service beforehand. — [Hệ thống Pháp luật, Art. 3](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-bao-ve-quyen-loi-nguoi-tieu-dung-2023/dieu-3)
- Continuous services are those with a term of ≥3 months or no fixed term. — [Hệ thống Pháp luật, law index](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-bao-ve-quyen-loi-nguoi-tieu-dung-2023)
- **Art. 42 (continuous-service contracts):**
  - The contract must be in writing.
  - Standard-form contracts must state fees and how they are calculated.
  - The provider must notify the consumer **≥7 working days before the service expires** about paying to continue, and ≥7 working days before the contract ends.
  - Unless agreed otherwise, the consumer may terminate at any time by giving notice and pays only for service already used.
  - Source: [Hệ thống Pháp luật, Art. 42](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-bao-ve-quyen-loi-nguoi-tieu-dung-2023/dieu-42)
- **Art. 38(3):** if a remote-transaction business omits or misstates the mandatory information, the consumer may unilaterally terminate within 30 days of contracting (quoted in a retailer's policy page). — [Viettel Store policy](https://viettelstore.vn/chinh-sach-xu-ly-hop-dong.html)

With only a foreign invoice and a bank record, input VAT is not creditable, though the cost can still be a deductible CIT expense (Khánh Hòa tax ruling). — [Crowe Vietnam](https://www.crowe.com/vn/vi-vn/news/purchasing-chatgpt-of-openai-with-only-an-invoice)

  - Foreign suppliers need not register if all their VN revenue is withheld by third parties.
  - Withholding parties include VN business organizations that pay VAT by the credit method and domestic e-commerce/digital platforms with ordering and payment functions.
  - Sources: [Thư viện Pháp luật, Công văn 4862/CT-CS](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/cong-van/Thue-Phi-Le-Phi/Cong-van-4862-CT-CS-2026-gioi-thieu-diem-moi-Thong-tu-90-2026-TT-BTC-714594.aspx?tab=2); [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/cong-van-4862-ct-cs-2026-cua-cuc-thue-gioi-thieu-diem-moi-va-trien-khai-thong-tu-90-2026-tt-btc-440470-d6.html)
- **Card FX fees:** SCB said on 30 Jul 2026 its foreign-currency conversion fee on international cards is unchanged (no % given). — [VNBA](https://vnba.org.vn/vi/scb-dieu-chinh-thong-tin-noi-dung-thu-phi-chuyen-doi-ngoai-te-10853.htm)
- Techcombank's historical Visa FX fee was 3.49% including VAT (2011) [old]. — [Techcombank](https://techcombank.com/thong-tin/thong-bao/thong-bao-dieu-chinh-chi-phi-quan-ly-chuyen-doi-chi-tieu-ngoai-te-cua-the-visa-phat-hanh-boi-techcombank)

### Inferences
- **Thresholds:** at 39k/month, the 1-billion threshold equals ~25,600 paid months/year (≈2,140 year-round subscribers). At 99k it is ≈840 subscribers. A beta and early launch run as a household business would likely owe no VAT or PIT and need no e-invoices. Revenue tracking, account notification and (if confirmed) annual revenue reporting still apply.
- **Registration timing:** once sales are regular, registration is the safe course, because the "low-income services" exemption is narrow and conditional business lines are excluded. A household business is cheap and fast; an LLC fits multiple co-founders and also gets the new ≤1-billion CIT exemption. Enterprises remain in the VAT system, so the "software service" vs "AI service, 10%" classification matters for a company [verify].
- **E-commerce checklist for the team's website:**
  - Publish operator details, the privacy policy, terms, complaint handling, prices and fees, payment policy, service-delivery and termination policy, and refund policy, all in Vietnamese.
  - Add an explicit "I agree" step at account creation.
  - Keep order and contract records ≥1 year (as a household/micro business) or ≥3 years otherwise.
  - Ask the provincial Department of Industry and Trade whether the online-ordering site must be notified [verify].
- **Auto-renewal design:** "no silent auto-renewal" is close to what Art. 42 already requires. Remind users ≥7 working days before a paid period expires, let them cancel at any time, and charge only for what was used.
- **Data protection:** if reading history and story memory count as "behavioural tracking" sensitive data, the household/micro/startup exemptions would not apply. The team would then need explicit, specific consent, an impact-assessment dossier, a DPO and possibly cross-border transfer assessments (e.g., for a foreign cloud host or sending account-linked data to foreign APIs). Design to minimize this:
  - store reading position locally where possible;
  - send no user identifiers to model vendors;
  - get a legal opinion before launch.
- **Paying OpenRouter/Anthropic as a VN entity:** if a VN company pays an unregistered foreign vendor, it likely has to withhold and pay FCT itself. That adds roughly 5–10% to model cost, so check the GDT list for Anthropic/OpenRouter. For household businesses or individuals paying by card, no withholding mechanism was found; costs are the card's FX fee plus any VAT the vendor charges.

### Gaps
- The exact notification procedure for a direct-business platform under Decree 248/2026 (forms, authority, deadline, fee) was not found. Whether an individual or household website must notify, and what the old online.gov.vn process became, also needs checking with the Department of Industry and Trade or a lawyer.
- Exact 2026 VAT rate for household services (historically 5% under Circular 40/2021; not re-verified for 2026). Also unconfirmed: whether PIT applies only above the threshold, and the revenue-notification form and deadline for households under 1 billion (reported by blogs as 01/TKN-CNKD by 31 Jan; not verified).
- Whether a student team selling subscriptions qualifies for any registration exemption; whether machine translation counts as a conditional business line. Copyright and IP risk of a paid service translating raw manga was not researched and should go to a lawyer.

## 4. Which pricing structures work in Vietnam?

### Takeaway
Leading services in Vietnam combine:
- low monthly anchors (33k–79k);
- one-time prepaid multi-month packs with no auto-renewal (Spotify sells 1/3/6/12-month packs; annual ≈ 10× monthly);
- heavily discounted student plans (Spotify Student ≈ 50% off; Google AI Plus free for a year to students);
- annual discounts of ~16–50%;
- sachet or short-period packs in telecom (daily, 3-day and 7-day data packs; possibly Spotify Mini).

Game spending runs on 20k/50k/100k top-up denominations with small bonuses at higher tiers. Payments skew to e-wallets and QR, and account sharing is high. A survey puts average digital-entertainment spend at 100k–500k/month, with half of respondents saying they will pay for quality content. No VN-specific conversion data comparing weekly vs monthly plans, referral credits or non-expiring credits was found.

### Inferences
- **Keep "prepaid, no silent renewal" as the default.** It matches Spotify's VN prepaid packs, telecom habits and VietQR's push-payment nature.
  - Offer 1-month and 3-month passes plus a 12-month pass. A ~17–30% annual discount sits inside the observed range (Spotify ~17%, Canva ≥16%, SePay 20–30%, Duolingo ~50%).
  - Make renewal opt-in through MoMo tokenization later.
- **Top-up denominations:** match game-card habits (20k / 50k / 100k) with a small bonus at 100k, e.g., 25k per 1,000 pages → 100k for 4,400 pages. Don't use odd amounts. Prepaid page credits that never expire are consistent with "owned after cancel" norms (Fonos), but no VN evidence on expiry practice was found.
- **Short pass:** a 7-day "binge" pass (e.g., 15k–20k) would mirror telecom 7-day packs and suit chapter-release bursts. Price it so 4 weekly passes cost more than "Đọc" monthly; daily data packs show buyers accept that sachets cost more per day.
- **Student and group:**
  - A student price ~50% off (e.g., ~19–25k) follows Spotify's VN pattern. Verification is costly, so a university-email check may suffice.
  - Given the high account sharing, a "group" plan (e.g., 3 accounts sharing a page pool) may convert better than policing shared logins.
- **Free tier:** a no-card free trial (100 pages + 30/month) is itself a differentiator. Spotify requires a payment method for trials, while VietQR users have no card on file.

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese data on conversion or retention by structure (weekly vs monthly vs annual, referral credits, non-expiring credits), and no evidence on Zing MP3, Nhaccuatui, FPT Play, VieON, Galaxy Play, Waka or Fonos conversion rates. Q&Me, Decision Lab and Appota reports with such figures were not found in accessible form.

## 5. How do Chrome/Edge extensions monetize without Chrome Web Store payments, and are there Vietnamese examples?

### Takeaway
The Chrome Web Store dropped its built-in payments and licensing in 2020–2021, so paid extensions now run their own accounts or licenses with an external checkout. Common options are ExtensionPay (Stripe-based, 5% fee), merchant-of-record checkouts (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo), or a web checkout tied to the developer's own user accounts. For a Vietnam-based team, Stripe-dependent tools are blocked. The workable pattern is the product's own login, with credits or passes bought on the website via VietQR/MoMo and checked by the extension against the backend. No Vietnamese paid-extension example was found.

### Cited Findings
- Google deprecated Chrome Web Store payments:
  - Developers charging through it, or using the UserLicenses/Payments/InAppProducts APIs or buy.js, had to move to another payment processor.
  - The licensing API would eventually stop reporting license status, and subscriptions would stop auto-renewing.
  - Sources: [Chrome developer docs](https://developer.chrome.com/webstore/cws-payments-deprecation) (page returned 404 when fetched; content via search snippet); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/google-kills-off-paid-for-chrome-extensions); [BGR, 23 Sep 2020](https://bgr.com/2020/09/23/google-chrome-browser-extensions-payments-support-deprecated/)
  - The ban was later reported as permanent. — [i-programmer](https://i-programmer.info/news/87-web-development/14032-googles-ban-on-paid-chrome-extensions-now-permanent.html)
- **ExtensionPay:**
  - Adds the open-source ExtPay.js and connects the developer's Stripe account.
  - Fee is 5% at time of charge with no monthly fee.
  - Supports subscriptions (monthly/quarterly/yearly), one-time payments and trials. Users log in with an email link instead of license keys.
  - Works in Chrome, Firefox, Edge and other Chromium browsers.
  - Source: [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com/)
- Stripe does not support Vietnam. — [stripe.com/global](https://stripe.com/global)
- MoR alternatives that pay out to VN: Polar (via Stripe Connect Express), Lemon Squeezy (bank payouts), Dodo Payments. Paddle does not exclude VN. — [Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries); [Lemon Squeezy](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries); [Dodo](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories.md); [Paddle](https://learn.profitwell.com/help/legal/sanctions/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle)
- **Manga-translation extensions seen:**
  - "Manga Translator" is listed as free, ad-free and registration-free. — [ExtensionAuditor](https://extensionauditor.com/scan/manga-translator-extension-hmaabgbekleghmkafncnneekppmnadpj)
  - Search results describe an extension ("IsManga") that lists Vietnamese among its target languages; its pricing was not found [secondary].

### Inferences
- **Recommended pattern for this project:**
  - The extension uses the same account as the web reader (OAuth or email link).
  - Entitlements (pass expiry, credit balance) live on the server.
  - Purchases happen only on the website: VietQR via payOS/SePay webhooks at beta, MoMo/ZaloPay added at launch, and optionally a MoR checkout for international users priced in USD.
  - The extension never handles payment data. This also keeps BYOK and local modes free of any billing dependency.
- License keys add support burden. The account-entitlement approach (as ExtensionPay does without keys) fits better with per-series story memory synced to the account.

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese paid Chrome or Edge extension with published VND pricing was found. Competitor manga-translation extension prices (e.g., credit-based tools) were not collected here.
- Microsoft Edge Add-ons store payment policy was not verified.
- The exact Chrome Web Store deprecation dates were not retrieved from the official page (404); news coverage dates the announcement to Sep 2020.
