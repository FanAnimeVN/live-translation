# Vietnamese manga readers: consumption, raw/MT demand and willingness to pay (research as of 21 Oct 2026)

Evidence grades used on every bullet: **[M n=…]** measured survey with sample size; **[M-panel]** traffic/panel estimate (Similarweb, ABJ/Photonic); **[P]** observed official price or store listing (fetched Oct 2026 unless dated); **[R]** reported by press/officials without underlying data; **[A]** anecdotal (forum posts, single interviews); **[V]** vendor/reseller self-claim; **[U]** our own earlier assumption (`docs/COST-PRICING.md`). "(snippet)" = number seen only in a search-result summary because the page itself was blocked (VOZ returns HTTP 403 to the fetcher; Reddit is not reachable by the tools; advertisingvietnam.com 403). Piracy sites are named as market facts only and written as `code` so they do not auto-link.

## 1. How do Vietnamese readers consume manga/manhwa/manhua in 2025–2026 (licensed print/digital, unlicensed sites, audience size)?

### Takeaway
Current-series reading in Vietnam is dominated by free unlicensed Vietnamese-language sites (plus Facebook pages) and, to an unmeasured degree, English-language aggregators. Measured scale: ~120 million monthly visits from Vietnam to manga piracy sites (ABJ/Photonic, June 2025; Vietnam ranks 5th in the world). Licensed print (Kim Đồng, IPM, NXB Trẻ, AMAK) serves collectors and children's classics, and licensed digital in Vietnamese is thin: Kim Dong Comics, MANGA Plus (Vietnamese UI, 29k/99k VND) and MangaToon (47k VND VIP) exist, and only MangaToon shows a sizeable user base. Enforcement rose sharply in 2026: a CODA office in HCMC, the first criminal cases, and a government takedown campaign.

### Inferences
- **Audience size (our rough estimate, not measured):** 120.2M monthly visits from Vietnam to manga piracy sites corresponds to about 8M, 4M or 2M monthly readers if a typical reader makes 15, 30 or 60 visits a month. This covers all languages read from Vietnam, including English and raw sites. The visits-per-reader figure is unknown, so treat this only as an order of magnitude (millions, not tens of millions).
- Licensed Vietnamese digital manga has a small footprint. Kim Dong Comics (195 ratings) and MANGA Plus (191 ratings) are tiny next to MangaToon (~42K ratings), so most readers have no habit of paying for digital manga. The 29k/47k price points show what licensed platforms think the market bears.
- MANGA Plus prices Vietnam in its discounted regional tier. 29k ≈ US$1.1 and 99k ≈ US$3.8, using the ~26,100 VND/USD implied by OpenAI's 522,500đ for US$20 ([BestApp](https://bestapp.vn/blog/chatgpt-plus-cho-sinh-vien-2026-go-vs-plus-share)). This is an independent signal that rights holders see Vietnam as a low-price market.
- The 2026 enforcement momentum (CODA office, criminal cases, a state campaign, Kim Đồng going public about losses) raises two risks. Vietnamese scanlation sites may become less reliable, which pushes some readers toward English aggregators or raw pages plus machine translation. The product may also be framed as a piracy enabler: product positioning should stress that it translates what the user's own tab already shows, with no hosting and no image caching (consistent with AGENTS.md privacy rules).
- TVM Comics, named in the brief, appears to be inactive since 2019, so active licensors are Kim Đồng, IPM, NXB Trẻ, AMAK/Uranix (BL/GL) and others.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Similarweb visit counts for the largest Vietnamese-language sites (TruyenQQ and the NetTruyen clones): domains rotate, and the free Similarweb pages show only the top 5 per category. A TorrentFreak snapshot quoted in a search summary (`truyenqqvn.com` ~43.1M visits, ~98% from Vietnam) could not be located or dated, so it is not used.
- Facebook scanlation-group and fan-page member counts could not be retrieved (Facebook is not fetchable).
- App download counts by country (MangaToon VN, MANGA Plus VN) are not public. Store rating counts are only weak proxies.
- Comico and Piccoma: no evidence found of Vietnamese-language services (not deeply searched). The MANGA Plus Vietnamese launch date was not found.
- No 2023–2026 survey of manga reading time or frequency in Vietnam was found. The only manga-specific survey (Q&Me n=300) is undated.

## 2. Demand for raw/faster chapters: how many wait for Vietnamese scanlations, read English scans, or already use machine translation?

### Takeaway
No measured share exists for Vietnam: there is no survey of waiting vs reading English vs reading raw. The evidence is indirect:
- Supply is strong: 88 Vietnamese-language pirate sites, and popular titles are translated soon after the Japanese release.
- English aggregators appear in Vietnam's Similarweb category rankings (noisy).
- Readers widely accept machine "convert" translation for Chinese web novels.
- Several Vietnamese forum tools and global extensions already offer AI comic translation, some with bring-your-own-key.

Demand for raw translation is therefore real but probably concentrated in long-tail, dropped and niche series and among impatient readers of hot series. That is an inference to test, not a fact.

### Inferences
- Reading in raw today is mostly a power-user behaviour: forum tools, BYOK and extensions. A mass behaviour would leave measurable traces such as Vietnamese-language MT apps with large rating counts, and none were found.
- Demand is likely concentrated in three groups:
  - series with no active Vietnamese group (dropped, niche, older or long-tail);
  - impatient readers in the 1–7 days before a Vietnamese scanlation appears for hot series;
  - readers currently reading English scans who would prefer Vietnamese.

  For the median reader of popular titles, free Vietnamese scanlations already arrive quickly. The earlier "50% raw" pricing scenario is unsupported for the median payer.
- The convert culture is the best local analogue: Vietnamese readers accept imperfect machine translation when it buys coverage and speed. They do complain about names, pronouns and address terms, which matches the product's story-memory and xưng hô rules.
- Free substitutes set the floor: Google Lens, free ChatGPT/Gemini on screenshots, IsManga's 80 free credits/week. The differentiators worth paying for are in-place lettering, continuity, and names and pronouns that stay consistent.

### Gaps
- No survey on the share of Vietnamese readers who wait for scanlations, read English, read raw, or use Google Lens/ChatGPT/Gemini on screenshots.
- VOZ thread bodies (dates, reply counts) could not be fetched (403); Reddit r/vietnam is not reachable by the tools; TikTok/YouTube comments are not searchable with the available tools.
- No Vietnam-only install counts for MT extensions or apps.
- An interview study on student motives for pirated comics (10 respondents; cited motives: free, complete, faster updates; frustrations: fees, region locks, languages) was found, but its country is unconfirmed, so it is not used. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/392228465_ANALYSIS_OF_STUDENT_MOTIVES_ON_ACCESSING_PIRATED_DIGITAL_COMICS)

## 3. Willingness to pay for digital entertainment among Vietnamese Gen Z/students (subscriptions, spend, thresholds, packs, discounts, sharing)

### Takeaway
The most-cited source (Appota 2024–2025) says ~50% would pay for personalised premium content and that average spend is 100k–500k VND/month across all digital entertainment, but it does not disclose its sample. Panel data show strong price sensitivity and sharing:
- 37% of non-subscribers say they can watch elsewhere free or cheaper, and 35% call subscriptions too expensive.
- ~18% use a family member's account.
- 53% of a 2025 sample used video piracy sites.

Students are budget-constrained: 55% spend under 3M VND/month in total. Account sharing extends to AI tools, with shared ChatGPT slots sold at tens of thousands of VND per person. Observed price anchors cluster around 29k–65k for single-purpose content and ~132k for AI subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- [M, sample not disclosed] Appota report "Nhu cầu Giải trí số Việt Nam 2024–2025" (released 5 Jun 2025, with Soi.Pro):
  - ~50% would pay for premium **if content is personalised**.
  - Average digital-entertainment spend is **100,000–500,000 VND/month**.
  - Gen Z spends **5–7 h/day** on digital entertainment, including TikTok 58 min/day and mobile games 26 min/day; Millennials 3–5 h/day.
  - 80% had moved to digital payments by 2024 (MoMo, ZaloPay, AppotaPay).
  - Paid services named: Netflix, Spotify Premium, Apple Music, Zing MP3 VIP, YouTube Premium.

### Inferences
- Measured price sensitivity is high and consistent across sources: piracy use is 53–61%, 35–37% cite price or free alternatives, ~18% use family accounts, and students mostly live on 1–3M VND/month. Relative to budgets:
  - **39k** is 1.3–3.9% of a 1–3M student budget, and 8–39% of Appota's 100–500k "entertainment wallet".
  - **99k** is 3.3–9.9% of that student budget, and 20–99% of the wallet.
- Observed thresholds:
  - ~30k: MANGA Plus Standard, Spotify Student, MangaToon annual equivalent.
  - ~50k: MangaToon VIP 47k; Q&Me's "≤50k" payer bucket.
  - ~65–100k: Spotify Individual, MANGA Plus Deluxe 99k.
  - ~130k: ChatGPT Go and Google AI Plus.

  Nothing found shows Vietnamese consumers paying more than 100k/month for a single-purpose comics service.
- Account sharing should be assumed by design. A 99k plan with 8,000 pages is splittable by 3–4 friends at ~25–33k each, which matches the shared-ChatGPT pattern. Per-device or per-profile reading caps, or an explicit "nhóm/duo" plan, should be considered.
- Annual discounts of ~30% match MangaToon (399k vs 47k×12 = 564k, i.e. 29% off), so the earlier 329k/year (≈30% off 39k×12) is consistent with market practice.
- No evidence was found either way for daily or weekly packs, but micro-transactions (10k–29k) are common in comics apps. Weekly passes should be tested, not assumed.

## 5. Assessment of the earlier assumptions (segments, WTP medians, 39k/1,000 + 99k/8,000 plans) and what a 20–50-person beta must measure

### Takeaway
The earlier model was almost entirely assumption, and the new evidence changes it only partly:
- **Supported:** a sub-50k entry price.
- **Corrected:** the MangaToon anchor is 47k in the VN store, not $1.99 (~52k).
- **Partly confirmed:** the Appota claim, though its method is opaque.
- **Unknown:** segment shares, usage intensities and the "50% raw" share.
- **Not supported:** the heavy and binge WTP medians (120k/160k), which sit above every manga-specific anchor and in the 132k AI-subscription band.

99k is defensible only as a ceiling (= MANGA Plus Deluxe, below ChatGPT Go). The beta has to measure page consumption and real payment behaviour, not stated intent alone.

**Implications for price points and plan shapes (to test, not adopt)**
- Keep the entry price at **29–39k** (MANGA Plus 29k, Spotify Student 33k, MangaToon 47k). Test 29k vs 39k vs 49k rather than higher.
- Test the heavy tier at **69–79k as well as 99k**: 99k collides with MANGA Plus Deluxe and sits at 75% of ChatGPT Go. Expect sharing at 99k, and decide whether to allow a declared duo or group plan (per-profile story memory) rather than fight it.
- Offer **short passes and top-ups** (for example a weekly pass, or 10–25k per 500–1,000 pages) to match micro-transaction habits and students' irregular reading. This is untested and needs measurement.
- Free tier: a competitor already gives 80 AI pages per week free, so a stingy free tier will be compared against that.
- Student segment: those with free Gemini Pro may prefer BYOK or local modes; keep BYOK prominent and cheap.

**What a 20–50-person beta must measure (design)**
1. **Screener / recruitment quotas.** At least 30% students. A mix of current sources: Vietnamese scanlation only, English scans, raw or MT users. Do not recruit only power users from VOZ. Record:
   - monthly disposable spend, using Cốc Cốc's brackets (<1M, 1–3M, 3–5M, >5M) for comparability;
   - current paid subscriptions, including ChatGPT Go, Google AI Plus or the student Gemini offer, MangaToon and MANGA Plus;
   - whether they share or buy any account slots, and at what price;
   - MT tools already used (Google Lens, ChatGPT or Gemini on screenshots, IsManga or similar).
2. **Usage telemetry over 3–4 weeks (opt-in, text-only):**
   - pages translated per user per day and active days per month; report p50/p80/p95;
   - the share of pages with no Vietnamese translation available at that time (needs a self-tag or a "why raw?" prompt: not translated yet / dropped / prefer faster / reading English);
   - pages per minute;
   - number of series followed.

   This replaces the minute-based segment guesses directly. Check what share of light users fit in 1,000 pages and what share of heavy users fit in 8,000.
3. **Van Westendorp (4 questions) at the end of week 2**, after users have seen real quality. Ask about a concrete offer ("a month of translation covering how much you read now"), adding Newton–Miller–Smith purchase-likelihood questions at two prices. With n=20–50, report the acceptable range (PMC–PME) and optimal price as indicative only.
4. **Real conversion test in week 3.**
   - Paywall with real local payment (MoMo, ZaloPay or VietQR).
   - Randomised price arms, for example 29k/39k/49k for the entry plan and 69k/79k/99k for the heavy plan.
   - A plan-shape choice: monthly vs weekly pass vs top-up vs annual.

   Statistical power is low: with 25 per arm, a 20% conversion has a ±16 pp 95% interval. Use the beta for direction and plan shape, and run the price A/B at public launch with ≥150–200 users per arm.
5. **Substitution and sharing probes.**
   - At X price, would you instead wait for a Vietnamese scanlation, read English, or use ChatGPT/Gemini on screenshots?
   - Would you share this account, with how many people, and would you pay for a duo or group plan?
   - Observe multi-device use in telemetry.
6. **Quality threshold.** Have users rate sample pages: natural Vietnamese, names and xưng hô (address terms) kept consistent, lettering. Ask the minimum quality at which they would pay, since VOZ users' main complaints about AI translation are names and pronouns.
7. **Short interviews (5–8 people)** to explain outliers: heavy readers, BYOK users, people who refused to pay.

### Gaps
- No Vietnam-specific data exists on reading-time distribution among *payers*, manga-specific WTP after 2023, the share of raw reading, or preference for weekly vs monthly passes. Only the beta (or a larger survey) can settle these.
- The only manga-specific WTP survey (Q&Me n=300) has no year, and the Appota and Rakuten sample sizes are not visible. A small paid survey (Q&Me or Cint panel, n≈300–500 manga readers aged 15–30) would give a better population baseline than the beta, at a cost not researched here.
- Conversion rates from free to paid for comics or MT tools in Vietnam were not found. No benchmark is used, to avoid inventing one.
